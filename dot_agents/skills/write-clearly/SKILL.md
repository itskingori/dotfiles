# Write Clearly

Help the reader understand the point, why it matters and what to do with it. Improve the writing without replacing the author's judgement or voice.

## Respect The Writing Context

Follow explicit task, repository, platform and genre guidance before this skill. Use this skill as a clarity layer within those constraints.

Preserve:

- facts, qualifications and degrees of certainty
- the author's perspective, tone and deliberate rhythm
- quotations, links, commands, code, identifiers and canonical names
- required structure, terminology and formatting

Do not invent support, close genuine ambiguity or strengthen a claim merely to make the prose sound decisive. Flag missing reasoning or evidence instead.

## Choose The Mode

- **Draft:** Turn source material into clear prose. Distinguish supplied facts from inference and do not fill factual gaps by guessing.
- **Revise:** Return improved prose that preserves meaning and intent. Explain only material editorial choices unless the user asks for detailed notes.
- **Audit:** Diagnose where comprehension breaks down. Do not rewrite or edit the source unless asked. Separate material issues from optional refinements, note effective choices worth preserving and use small suggested rewrites only where they make an issue concrete.

When the requested mode is clear, begin the work without asking the user to confirm it.

## Shape The Explanation

1. Identify the intended reader, the question they need answered and any action they must take.
2. Find the main point and the reasoning, evidence or mechanism that supports it.
3. Put the outcome or key information early when the format calls for it. Preserve deliberate narrative discovery in essays and other reflective writing.
4. Give each paragraph one job. Place qualifications and constraints next to the claim they limit.
5. Prefer concrete actors, specific verbs and precise nouns. Define unfamiliar terms before relying on them.
6. Remove repetition, filler and pre-announcements that do not help the reader understand or act.
7. Check that headings, lists, examples and transitions reveal the structure rather than merely decorate it.

Read [references/technical-writing.md](references/technical-writing.md) when the work includes developer documentation, procedures, reference material, architecture or design explanations, code examples or other technical content.

## Preserve Voice

Clarity is not uniformity. Do not automatically remove first-person perspective, sentence variation, humour, metaphor, rhetorical contrast or a gradual reveal when those choices serve the piece and remain understandable.

For personal or editorial writing, distinguish a comprehension problem from a stylistic preference. A specialised blog or publication guide controls voice and composition when one is available.

## Review The Result

Before returning the work, check:

- Can the intended reader identify the point without private or missing context?
- Does each claim have enough nearby explanation, evidence or grounding?
- Are actors, actions, conditions and consequences unambiguous?
- Did the edit preserve meaning, uncertainty and voice?
- Is the structure easy to scan without becoming mechanical?
- Did any sentence become smoother but less accurate?
